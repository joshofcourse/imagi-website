#### What Is a Cookie

A cookie is a small text file that is stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. Cookies may be "first-party cookies," which are set by the website you are visiting, or "third-party cookies," which are set by external services (such as analytics or advertising providers) operating on the website you are visiting.

#### Where We Place Cookies

We may place cookies in the following locations:

-   On our websites.
-   On sites we host for our users.
-   In the emails we send.

We use the following categories of cookies:

#### Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are required for the operation of our website. They include cookies that enable core functionality such as shopping cart operation, secure checkout, and session management. Without these cookies, the website cannot function properly. Because these cookies are essential, they cannot be disabled.

#### Functionality Cookies

These cookies help us remember your preferences and settings when you revisit our website, such as your language selection, currency preference, or video playback settings. They improve your browsing experience but are not strictly necessary for the website to function.

#### Analytics and Performance Cookies

These cookies collect information about how visitors interact with our website, such as which pages are visited most often and whether visitors encounter error messages. We use this information to improve the performance of our website and to better understand how visitors use it. The data collected by these cookies is aggregated and does not directly identify individual visitors. We use services such as Google Analytics and Shopify Analytics for this purpose.

#### Advertising Cookies

These cookies may be used to deliver advertisements that are relevant to you and to measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns. They remember that you have visited our website and may share this information with advertising platforms. We may use third-party advertising services, such as those provided by Google and social media platforms, which set their own cookies. These cookies track browsing behavior across websites to build a profile of your interests and show you relevant advertisements elsewhere.

#### Third-Party Cookies

Our website may include embedded content and integrations from third-party services, such as YouTube videos and social media sharing buttons. These services may set their own cookies, which are governed by the respective third party's privacy and cookie policies. We do not have direct control over these cookies. We encourage you to review the cookie and privacy policies of any third-party services you interact with through our website.

You have the right to accept or refuse cookies from our website at any time. You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings. Below are links to cookie management instructions for common browsers:

Please note: If cookies are disabled, some features of the website may not operate as intended.

#### Cookie Consent

When you first visit our website, you will be presented with a cookie consent banner that allows you to accept or manage your cookie preferences. You can change your preferences at any time through your browser settings.

For visitors in the European Economic Area (EEA) and other jurisdictions that require consent, non-essential cookies will not be placed until you provide your consent.
