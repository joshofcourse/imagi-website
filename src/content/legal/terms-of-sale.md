We fundamentally believe you will be thrilled with the products you purchase from imagi Education. However, we understand that sometimes a product may not be what you expected it to be.

#### 1.2. How to Return an Item

To return your item, send us an email clearly indicating the item you intend to return or the order you intend to cancel. Please reach out to us at support@imagilabs.com. We will review your request, and if it is approved, we will send you instructions for how to proceed with the return.

We offer free returns and refund all shipping costs except additional costs for express delivery.

#### 1.3. Refund

Once we have received and inspected your item, we will send you an email to notify you that we have received your returned item and confirm the status of your return. If the refund is approved, a refund is initiated immediately.

If you cancel an order before it is fulfilled, and you were billed for it, we initiate your refund immediately after you submit your cancellation request. The way your refund is processed depends on your original payment method. If you paid by credit or debit card, refunds will be sent to the card-issuing bank within 14 business days of receipt of the returned item or cancellation request.

#### 1.4. Late or Missing Refunds

If you have not received a refund yet, first check your bank account again. It may take some time to receive the refund. If the refund was issued but you still have not received it, please contact your bank. There is often some processing time before a refund is posted.

If you have done all of this and have not received your refund, please contact us at support@imagilabs.com.

#### 1.5. Exchange

We only exchange items if they are defective or damaged. If you need to exchange your item, please send us an email at support@imagilabs.com. We will review your request, and if it is approved, we will send you instructions on how to proceed with the exchange.

#### 1.6. Warranty

imagi products are covered by a three-year warranty against any defects in materials or workmanship. The three-year period starts from the day the product is received. In order to claim the warranty, you will need to provide proof of purchase. Without proof of purchase, no exchange or refund can be provided.

Once the product is received, imagi Education will examine it and if defects are found, an exchange or refund will be processed. However, if the defect appears because of misuse, modification, or unauthorized repair, the warranty will not be valid. The warranty is not transferable.
