---
question: "A student's project has disappeared. Can it be recovered?"
group: troubleshooting
order: 2
---

Projects are saved automatically in Lovable. If a student can't find their work, make sure they're logged into the correct account. If the project was published, it can be accessed via the published link. For anything else, contact [support@imagilabs.com](mailto:support@imagilabs.com).
