---
question: "Lovable previews aren't loading. What should I do?"
group: troubleshooting
order: 3
---

This usually means your school network is blocking \*.lovable.app. Ask your IT team to allow \*.lovable.app, including WebSocket connections on port 443. It's needed both for previews inside imagi and for published projects. If previews still don't load, email support@imagilabs.com and we'll help.
