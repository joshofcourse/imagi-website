---
question: "A student can't log in. What should I do?"
group: troubleshooting
order: 1
---

Check that you've shared the correct username and password from your imagi Edu dashboard. Students use teacher-generated credentials. They don't create their own login, and they shouldn't use lovable.dev. If you're still having trouble, contact [support@imagilabs.com](mailto:support@imagilabs.com).
