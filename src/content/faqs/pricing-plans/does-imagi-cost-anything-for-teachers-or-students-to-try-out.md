question: "Does imagi cost anything for teachers or students to try out?"
group: pricing-plans
order: 1
---

Teacher access to imagi Edu is always free.  
You can explore the platform and build your own projects at any time. Verified teachers (who aren’t already part of a district or on a Pro plan) get 25 free Lovable trial credits to try it out for themselves. Use a school email to get verified quicker.    
  
Student access is enabled during free campaign windows or year-round with an imagi Edu Pro subscription.  
  

For our different pricing options, find out more on our [pricing page.](/pricing)[](/pricing)
