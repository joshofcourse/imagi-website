The imagiCharm is a programmable device that brings creative projects and designs coded in Python to life. With its 64 colorful LED lights and durable make, it can be used as a powerful way to make learning how to code more creative, tangible, and fun. The imagiCharm connects via Bluetooth with the imagi Edu platform and apps for schools or the imagi coding game for iPhone and iPad for at-home use.
