imagi provides a safe, educational layer between AI tools and students.  
  
First, we equip students with essential **computer science foundations,** alongside **computational thinking skills**. We do this through creative Python coding projects.   
  
Then, we support them in **how to transfer these skills into an AI-driven world.** With our ready-made lesson materials and safe AI tool integrations, students can practice their career-ready skills in creative projects.   
  
We are helping students to create with AI, not cheat with AI.
