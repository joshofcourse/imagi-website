---
question: "How do students log in to start building apps?"
group: getting-started
order: 2
---

Students log in using the anonymous username and password you share from your dashboard. [Set those up following these steps.](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Klgo9yFVVWsLawhbFU4qHxLQyej0WuekW1vI7rb7KuA/edit?usp=sharing)   
  
They should only access Lovable via imagi;  they should not use the standard Lovable login at lovable.dev, which requires an email address and is not part of the safe classroom setup.
