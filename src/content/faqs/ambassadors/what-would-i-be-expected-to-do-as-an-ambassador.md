---
question: "What would I be expected to do as an Ambassador?"
group: ambassadors
order: 2
---

We hope you would: 

-   **Help shape what we build next** with your classroom experience, feedback, and ideas.
-   **Contribute to our community.** You'll connect with other teachers, learn from each other, and share wins.
-   **Test new features.** You can give feedback and even promote the new feature across your networks.
-   **Learn and develop together.** With guest speakers, hackathons, or training on specific imagi features.

We'd hope that you join regular webinars (no more than once a month) and share examples of your projects. These would be the minimum ask. Life happens, and we build around that, not the other way around.
