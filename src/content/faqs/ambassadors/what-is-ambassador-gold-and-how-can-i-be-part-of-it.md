question: "What is Ambassador GOLD and how can I be part of it?"
group: ambassadors
order: 10
---

Ambassador GOLD is a smaller, more involved group of around 10 people who will be selected after a couple of months into the normal Ambassador program. 

We will be looking out for Ambassadors who are happy to facilitate, confident in using imagi (and Lovable),  and who go that extra mile to support imagi's mission.

GOLD Ambassadors will be paid to lead PD sessions and represent imagi at conferences.  They'll receive additional training, extra Lovable credits, and a GOLD badge and certificate.

You don't need to apply separately to be a GOLD Ambassador, but you can opt in during the normal Ambassador application process.
