---
question: "What happens when a student reaches their limit?"
group: lovable
order: 10
---

They can't start a new prompt until you raise their limit. A prompt is charged once Lovable finishes building it, so a student's last prompt can take them slightly over their limit (for example, 5.1 of 5 credits).

If you're running low on credits for your students, email support@imagilabs.com to talk about more credits.
