---
question: "Can Lovable projects be shared for others to see?"
group: lovable
order: 7
---

Yes, there are two different ways.  
  
1\. Pressing the ‘Publish to web’ button creates a **public** URL for the app that **anyone** can open on the web. It will only show the finished app, not the code, prompts, or creator. We will always display a short pop-up before publishing, reminding you of what will become public so you can decide if you're ready.

2\. Alternatively, "Share to classroom" shows the whole project, including your prompts, only to logged-in students and teacher(s) who are part of the same classroom.

If the project is linked to a lesson, it will need teacher approval before it can be published or shared to the classroom. But personal (non-lesson) projects can be published or shared at any time.

Remember: if you don’t want students to create and publish personal projects outside of lessons, you can always turn Lovable off in your classroom settings.
