---
question: "Can apps be edited or unpublished from the web?"
group: lovable
order: 6
---

Yes and no; it depends on the type of project:  

-   **Personal** apps, created in your own project area, can be edited and republished at any time. Open the project back up, make your edits, then republish. The same public link automatically updates.  
      
    
-   **Lesson-linked** projects, assigned by the teacher, work a little differently. These are first sent to the teacher for their review, and then they can be published to the web. Because the teacher has added their final ‘stamp of approval’, it means that no other _edits_ can be made; the apps can only be unpublished.
