---
question: "What are AI credits, and how do they work?"
group: lovable
order: 8
---

Credits power Lovable builds. Each time a student or teacher generates or updates a project, credits are used. Your credits stay in your teacher account, and your students build from them up to the limit you set for each student.  
  
As responsible users of AI, we need to learn how to use credits responsibly and understand the ethical effects on our world. Our lesson plans and projects provide a hands-on way to learn about this.  

Credit top-ups for students are managed through your imagi Edu subscription. If your class is running low, reach out to the imagi team at [support@imagilabs.com](mailto:support@imagilabs.com) to discuss your options for buying more credits.
