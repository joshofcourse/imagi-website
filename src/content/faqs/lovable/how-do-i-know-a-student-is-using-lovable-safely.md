This version of Lovable is already made student-friendly by being inside imagi’s platform. We embed certain guardrails.

To give you a sense of control, you can toggle access to Lovable on and off at any time. In addition, our built-in safety monitor gives teachers the ability to track students’ AI use, flags any inappropriate prompts, with reasons why.

If you would like more specific guidance about how imagi can support your individual school’s safety needs, please contact us at [support@imagilabs.com](mailto:support@imagilabs.com).
