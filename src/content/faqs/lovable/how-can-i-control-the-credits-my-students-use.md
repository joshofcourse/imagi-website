---
question: "How can I control the credits my students use?"
group: lovable
order: 9
---

When your imagi account was purchased, the number of AI credits was decided. You now have control over how your students use them through **credit limits.**  
  
You can set one limit for the whole class or even set individual student limits. Students then build until they reach their limit. Any unused credits stay with you. Watch [this video](https://youtu.be/1dPf9txYbeM) to find out more.  
  
You can also add an optional end date to stop any credits being used past that date.  
  
Only the class owner can change limits and the end date. Co-teachers can see how much each student has used.
