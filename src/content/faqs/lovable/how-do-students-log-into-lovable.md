---
question: "How do students log into Lovable?"
group: lovable
order: 4
---

Students log in **via imagi**, using the anonymous username and password you share when you've added them to a classroom. Find instructions on our [resources page.](/resources)  
  
They should not use the standard Lovable login at lovable.dev, which requires an email address and is not part of the safe classroom setup.
