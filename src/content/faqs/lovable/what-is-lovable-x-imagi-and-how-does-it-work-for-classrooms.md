Lovable x imagi brings AI-assisted app creation into your classroom. Students describe an idea in plain language, and Lovable builds it into a working game, quiz, website, or app, no coding experience needed. imagi provides a safe, teacher-controlled doorway into Lovable, along with ready-made lesson materials to get your class up and running quickly.
