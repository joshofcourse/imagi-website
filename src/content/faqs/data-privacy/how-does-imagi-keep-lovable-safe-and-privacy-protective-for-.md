---
question: "How does imagi keep Lovable safe and privacy-protective for students?"
group: data-privacy
order: 1
---

Lovable x imagi is built for COPPA and FERPA compliance. Students log in through teacher-generated, anonymous accounts, so we don't collect a student's email address, date of birth, or other contact information. There are no social features and no ads, and AI content is moderated before it reaches students. Student data is not used to train AI, and we sign Data Processing Agreements with schools and districts.  
  
Important: students should never create their own account at lovable.dev. If they do, these protections no longer apply. They should only log in via imagi.
