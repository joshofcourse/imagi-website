Credits are like AI coins that power Lovable builds. Each time a student or teacher generates or updates a project, credits are used. 

As responsible users of AI, students must learn to use credits wisely and understand AI's effects on our world. Our lesson plans and projects give them a hands-on way to learn this.  
  
The free plan includes 25 credits for teachers to test out Lovable. valid for 30 days  
The teacher plan includes 2,500 credits to share amongst students.   
The school and district plan can have different total credit amounts. Get in touch.
