---
question: "What's the difference between the free and paid plans?"
group: pricing
order: 1
---

Free imagi Edu is for teachers trying out imagi. There's a selection of lesson plans, access for 50 students on the Python Coding Platform, and a handful of **teacher** credits to try AI building with Lovable.

imagi Edu Pro (Teacher) still covers 50 students, but also grants student-safe access to Lovable. You'll have 2,500 credits to allocate to students, so they can build apps with AI.  This package also comes with full access to our lesson plans.

imagi Edu Pro (Schools and Districts) is for schools and districts rolling out across many classrooms, and is priced by quote.
