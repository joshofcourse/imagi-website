---
question: "How can I control the credits my students use?"
group: pricing
order: 5
---

The number of AI credits is set when your imagi plan is purchased. After that, you decide how they're used. Credit allocation lets you distribute credits to a whole classroom or to certain students.

On a wider school/district plan, the admin can control the credit allocation amongst teachers. Teachers must give out credits before students can build.

Access to Lovable (and therefore credits) can be turned off at any time.
