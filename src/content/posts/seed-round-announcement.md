_Backed by Brighteye Ventures, DayOne Capital, and Will.i.am and partnering with Lovable and OpenAI, imagi is scaling its AI literacy platform, which has already put safe AI tools in the hands of 700k students_

  

NEW YORK / STOKHOLM. 23 July 2026

[imagi](//imagilabs.com) has raised a $4.5 million seed round to expand access to structured AI learning for students and teachers in the US and beyond. The round was led by Brighteye Ventures and [DayOne Capital](https://www.dayonecapital.com/), with participation from FemInvest and We Are Human, led by the founders of Kahoot.

  

Operating across more than 100 school districts in all US states, including the Hawaii Department of Education, imagi’s platform offers K-12 students direct, supervised access to AI tools, within a curriculum designed to build genuine AI literacy. By partnering with OpenAI and Lovable, imagi structures classroom AI teaching with safety guardrails, giving educators the support to confidently deploy these tools.

  

The funding round also includes a notable group of angels spanning founders, operators, and educators, including ElevenLabs founder Mati Staniszewski, former GitHub CEO Thomas Dohmke and Voi CEO and Co-founder Fredrik Hjelm. Singer Will.i.am and serial entrepreneurs Sophia Amoruso and Simon Squibb also invested in the over-subscribed round, alongside leaders from OpenAI, Spotify and Lovable. imagi also received investment from mission-aligned funders, including Morgan Stanley and Sparkmind Capital.

### **Building a safe choice for children and AI educators**  

Across various subjects including computer science, STEM, art, and even math, teachers are bringing creative AI-assisted coding into their classrooms. Students are using AI through the safe Lovable × imagi platform to build their own apps and projects, learning to create with AI rather than just cheat with it.

While research from [CollegeBoard](https://research.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ai-research-brief-1_vf.pdf) found that more than 85% of school administrators view AI education as valuable, 45% report that access to GenAI tools is restricted in the classroom. This is despite the fact that 84% of high school students use LLMs to help with schoolwork. Through partnerships with OpenAI and Lovable, imagi offers ready-to-teach AI lessons on the platforms that students are already familiar with.

imagi has already given more than 700,000 students in 140 countries access to safe, compliant and pedagogically sound AI. imagi has seen 30x year-on-year user growth and 3x ARR growth, with district partnerships now live in the US. imagi will use the latest funding to continue scaling across US K-12 education and integrate additional frontier AI tools. The company also plans to scale teacher-training programmes, team hiring with a focus on US-based talent, and investment in compliance and research to cement imagi as the default safe choice for school district administrators.

imagi was established by co-founders [Dora Palfi](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dorapalfi) (CEO) and [Beatrice Ionascu](https://www.linkedin.com/in/bionascu) (CTO), following a user-centred research project conducted at KTH University in Stockholm. The company doubled down on US K-12 education in 2024. The company is headquartered in New York with a team spanning the US, UK, Sweden, and Finland. In November 2025, imagi added [Sari Hurme-Mehtälä](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sariirene/) to its leadership team as Chief Growth Officer. Sari sold her edtech company Kide Science to Accelerate Learning, becoming the first Nordic edtech company to make an exit to the US.

[Dora Palfi](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dorapalfi), Co-founder and CEO of imagi comments: “Today's schoolchildren are the first generation who will never know a world without AI. This means AI education won’t just be the new ICT, but the new literacy. But unlike reading schemes, AI is not designed with children in mind. This means we need safe, tested AI education for children, with guardrails trusted by educators and parents. AI is accelerating faster than any technology before, which is why we cannot bury our heads in the sand, and ignore the need to introduce children to AI in a responsible way. Working already with Lovable and OpenAI, we’ve engaged hundreds of thousands of young people, and with our latest funding and ever-growing roster of partners, we hope to give millions more access to safe frontier AI tools.”
